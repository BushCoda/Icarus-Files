## How to Get Featured

We'd love to showcase your Icarus mods and projects! Here's how to get your project featured:

### Submission Requirements

Your project should:
- Be Icarus-related (mods, tools, guides, research, etc.)
- Have a public GitHub repository
- Include a clear README with instructions on how to use/install it
- Be reasonably active (not abandoned)

### How to Submit

**Option 1: Open an Issue**
- Go to the [Issues](https://github.com/BushCoda/Icarus-Files/issues) tab
- Create a new issue with the title: "Feature Request: [Your Project Name]"
- Include:
  - Your GitHub username
  - Your project name and repository link
  - A brief description (1-2 sentences)
  - The category it fits (Building Mods, Tools, Gameplay Mods, Guides, etc.)

**Option 2: Create a Pull Request**
- Fork this repository
- Add your project to the appropriate section in `COMMUNITY_SHOWCASE.md`
- Submit a pull request with details about your project

### What Happens Next

- We'll review your submission within 1-2 weeks
- If your project meets our guidelines, it will be added to the showcase
- **You maintain full ownership and control** of your repository
- When you update your project, your featured listing will automatically reflect your repository's current state

### Important Notes

- Featuring a project here is **not an endorsement** of the mod's functionality—it's purely a showcase
- Projects may be removed if they become abandoned (no updates for 6+ months) or if they violate GitHub's terms of service
- Please ensure your project complies with game modding policies and respects intellectual property
